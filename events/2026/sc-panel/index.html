Overview

How can quantum computing become an integral part of HPC infrastructure? This panel examines the move from separate quantum services and custom connectors to a unified environment for scheduling, workflows, and system monitoring. Representatives from research laboratories and industry will discuss how to design quantum-HPC integration from the outset as facilities deploy quantum resources.
